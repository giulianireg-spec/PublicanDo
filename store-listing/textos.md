🏅 COLECCIONÁ SELLOS Y GANÁ TÍTULOS
Cuando llegás a un lugar, abrí su ficha y recolectá su sello. Con tus sellos ganás títulos por localidad, provincia y categoría, como "Paisajista experto de Jujuy" o "Maestro historiador de Córdoba". Los festivales tienen un sello por edición, que solo se consigue durante el evento. Compartí tu colección como imagen.

🗺️ MAPA Y GUÍAS DE RECORRIDO
Mirá los lugares en el mapa o seguí guías armadas paso a paso: Buenos Aires en 2 días, Quebrada de Humahuaca, Bariloche y los Siete Lagos, El Calafate y El Chaltén, Iguazú y las misiones jesuíticas, y muchas más. También podés crear y compartir las tuyas.

📴 FUNCIONA SIN SEÑAL
GuianDo guarda los lugares cercanos para que sigas explorando en la montaña, la ruta o un parque nacional sin datos. Los sellos que recolectes sin conexión se validan solos cuando vuelve la señal.

🌐 EN ESPAÑOL, INGLÉS Y PORTUGUÉS

### Full description (max 4000)
🧭 GuianDo is your guide to discovering Argentina: national parks, small towns, museums, festivals, fairs and places to eat and stay, with photos, history, directions and practical tips for your visit. It is built by a community of travelers, locals and businesses from all over the country.

📍 ALL 23 PROVINCES AND THE CITY OF BUENOS AIRES
More than 200 tourist places plus almost 400 restaurants and places to stay in Córdoba, with more added every week:
• Patagonia: Perito Moreno Glacier, El Chaltén and Fitz Roy, Bariloche, Villa La Angostura, Península Valdés, Ushuaia.
• North: Quebrada de Humahuaca, Hill of Seven Colors, Salinas Grandes, Cafayate, Cachi, Train to the Clouds.
• Cuyo: Aconcagua, Puente del Inca, Valley of the Moon and Talampaya, Atuel Canyon.
• Littoral: Iguazú Falls, Iberá Wetlands, San Ignacio Miní, Gualeguaychú Carnival.
• Buenos Aires: Caminito, Teatro Colón, Recoleta, San Telmo Fair, Tigre, Mar del Plata.
• Córdoba: Jesuit Estancias, Cosquín, La Cumbrecita, Champaquí, Uritorco and hundreds more.

🏅 COLLECT STAMPS AND EARN TITLES
When you arrive somewhere, open its page and collect its stamp. Your stamps earn you titles for each town, province and category. Festivals have a stamp for each edition that you can only get during the event. Share your collection as an image.

🗺️ MAP AND ITINERARIES
See places on the map or follow step-by-step guides: Buenos Aires in 2 days, Quebrada de Humahuaca, Bariloche and the Seven Lakes, El Calafate and El Chaltén, Iguazú and the Jesuit missions, and many more. You can also create and share your own.

📴 WORKS OFFLINE
GuianDo saves nearby places so you can keep exploring in the mountains, on the road or in a national park without data. Stamps you collect offline are validated automatically when you're back online.

🌐 IN ENGLISH, SPANISH AND PORTUGUESE
The app and the place information are available in three languages.

🏪 LIST YOUR BUSINESS FOR FREE
Run a restaurant, a place to stay or a tourism business? List it for free with photos, opening hours, location and contact details, check your stats and print your QR kit so visitors can find you.

GuianDo is free. Discover, collect and share the best of Argentina.
